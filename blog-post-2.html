Understanding Active Noise Cancellation

Published on September 05, 2026 • 7 min read

Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) has become a staple in modern premium headphones, but the technology behind it is nothing short of computational magic.

The Physics of Silence

At its core, sound is a wave of air pressure. ANC works on the principle of destructive interference. Microphones on the outside (and inside) of the headphone earcups constantly monitor ambient noise.

Once this noise is detected, a digital signal processor (DSP) generates an inverted sound wave—an exact mirror image of the noise—and plays it through the speakers alongside your music. When the external noise wave and the internal anti-noise wave meet, they cancel each other out, leaving you with pure silence.
