Introducing the Aethereus Pro Max

Published on September 12, 2026 • 5 min read

Today marks a significant milestone for Aura Audio. After three years of rigorous research and development, we are thrilled to unveil our flagship wireless headphones: the Aethereus Pro Max.

Redefining the Acoustic Standard

The Pro Max features an entirely new 40mm dynamic driver, engineered in-house to deliver a frequency response that rivals wired studio monitors. But we didn't stop at the hardware. Driven by our custom H2 compute chip, the active noise cancellation adapts to your environment 200 times per second.

"Our goal wasn't just to make a great headphone. It was to completely erase the barrier between the listener and the music."

The Aethereus Pro Max is available today globally. Experience sound like never before.
