Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products.

Do Aura headphones support spatial audio?

Yes, both the Aethereus Pro Max and Studio models support fully immersive spatial audio with dynamic head tracking on compatible devices.

What is the warranty period?

All Aura products come with a standard 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.

How long does the battery last?

The Aethereus Pro Max provides up to 40 hours of listening time with Active Noise Cancellation enabled. A quick 10-minute charge gives you 5 hours of playback.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day, no-questions-asked return policy. If you aren't completely satisfied, simply return the product in its original packaging for a full refund.
