UX Personas

Target audience research for the Aura Audio UI/UX Project.

"The Audiophile Professional"

Alex, 32 • Sound Engineer

Goals

  • Requires flat frequency response for accurate mixing.
  • Wants all-day comfort for 8+ hour studio sessions.

Pain Points

  • Frustrated by "bassy" lifestyle brands.
  • Hates clumsy UI when trying to compare technical specs.

"The Remote Worker"

Sam, 28 • Tech Consultant

Goals

  • Needs top-tier ANC to block out coffee shop noise.
  • Wants a premium, sleek look for video calls.

Pain Points

  • Battery anxiety during long travel days.
  • Needs a seamless, fast checkout experience on mobile.
